In this section, you will find information regarding some of the most common neurological conditions that may require evaluation by a neurosurgeon. The objective is to provide a clear guide for patients experiencing symptoms related to the brain, the spine, or the nervous system.

Herniated disc

A herniated disc is one of the most common causes of back pain and pain that radiates into the arms or legs. A timely evaluation allows for determining whether treatment should be conservative or surgical.

Spine surgery

Spine surgery may be considered when persistent pain, nerve compression or neurological symptoms affect daily life and conservative treatment is not enough.

Sciatic pain occurs when there is compression of the sciatic nerve, generally caused by problems in the lumbar spine.

Spinal stenosis occurs when the vertebral canal narrows and compresses nerve structures, causing pain or weakness.
