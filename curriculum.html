About Dr. José Manuel Segovia

Dr. José Manuel Segovia is a board-certified neurosurgeon with decades of clinical experience. His approach focuses on careful evaluation, clear communication, and individualized treatment plans.

During consultations, Dr. Segovia prioritizes patient understanding, taking time to explain diagnoses, imaging findings, and treatment options in a clear and practical way.

  • Specialty in Neurosurgery – National Medical Center, IMSS.

Patient-Centered Approach
