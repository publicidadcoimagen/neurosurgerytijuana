Herniated Disc in Tijuana

A herniated disc occurs when one of the intervertebral discs of the spine shifts or ruptures, exerting pressure on the nerve roots. This can cause pain, tingling, weakness, or loss of sensation in the arms or legs.

Symptoms of a Herniated Disc

Symptoms may vary depending on the location of the hernia, but commonly include lower back pain, pain radiating into the leg (sciatica), muscle weakness, or numbness.

Diagnosis

Diagnosis is established through a medical history, neurological examination, and imaging studies such as magnetic resonance imaging.

Treatment

Many patients improve with conservative treatment. In some cases, when there is significant nerve compression or persistent symptoms, surgical treatment may be considered.
