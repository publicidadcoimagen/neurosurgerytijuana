Herniated Disc Treatment in Tijuana

A herniated disc is one of the most common causes of back pain and pain that radiates into the legs or arms. This condition occurs when one of the intervertebral discs in the spine moves or ruptures, placing pressure on nearby nerves.

Many patients search for information about herniated disc treatment in Tijuana when they experience persistent symptoms such as lower back pain, leg pain (sciatica), numbness or muscle weakness. A consultation with a neurosurgeon can help determine the exact cause and the most appropriate treatment.

What is a herniated disc?

Intervertebral discs act as cushions between the vertebrae of the spine. When the inner material of the disc protrudes outward, it can compress nearby nerves. This pressure may cause pain, numbness or weakness.

Herniated discs most commonly affect the lumbar or cervical spine. Age-related changes, repetitive movements, lifting heavy objects or injuries may increase the risk of developing this condition.

Common symptoms

Symptoms may vary depending on the location of the herniated disc. Common signs include:

  • Persistent lower back pain
  • Pain radiating down the leg (sciatica)
  • Numbness or tingling
  • Muscle weakness
  • Neck pain or arm pain

If these symptoms do not improve with conservative treatment or significantly affect daily life, a medical evaluation may be recommended.

Diagnosis

Diagnosis usually includes a detailed medical history, neurological examination and imaging studies. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) is commonly used to identify the location and severity of nerve compression.

Proper diagnosis helps rule out other causes of back pain and allows physicians to develop the most appropriate treatment plan.

Treatment options

Not all herniated discs require surgery. Many patients improve with conservative treatments such as medication, physical therapy or lifestyle adjustments.

In cases where nerve compression is severe or symptoms persist despite conservative treatment, surgery may be considered. The goal of surgery is to relieve pressure on the nerve and improve symptoms.
