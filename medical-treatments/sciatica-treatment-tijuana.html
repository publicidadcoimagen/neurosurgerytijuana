Sciatica pain: causes and treatment

Sciatica is a common condition related to irritation or compression of the sciatic nerve, the longest nerve in the human body. This nerve begins in the lower spine and extends through the hips, buttocks and legs.

Many patients search for information about sciatica treatment in Tijuana when experiencing lower back pain radiating into the leg, numbness, tingling or weakness. A medical evaluation helps identify the cause and determine the most appropriate treatment.

What is sciatica?

Sciatica is not a disease itself but a group of symptoms related to irritation of the sciatic nerve. It is frequently associated with lumbar spine conditions such as herniated discs or spinal stenosis.

Common symptoms

  • Lower back pain radiating into the leg
  • Tingling or burning sensation
  • Numbness in the leg or foot
  • Muscle weakness
  • Difficulty walking or sitting for long periods

Treatment options

Initial treatment often includes medication, physical therapy and activity modification. When symptoms persist, imaging studies such as MRI may be recommended to evaluate the spine.
