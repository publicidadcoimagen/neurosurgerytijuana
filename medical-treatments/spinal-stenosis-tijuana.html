Spinal stenosis in Tijuana

Spinal stenosis occurs when the spinal canal narrows and places pressure on nerves or the spinal cord. This condition often affects the lumbar or cervical spine and is commonly associated with degenerative changes over time.

Many patients search for spinal stenosis treatment in Tijuana when they experience back pain, numbness, weakness or progressive difficulty walking. A specialist evaluation helps identify the cause and determine the most appropriate treatment plan.

Common symptoms

  • Persistent back or neck pain
  • Leg pain or heaviness while walking
  • Numbness or tingling
  • Muscle weakness
  • Difficulty walking long distances
  • Partial relief when sitting or bending forward

Diagnosis

Diagnosis is typically based on medical history, neurological examination and imaging studies such as MRI or CT scan. These studies help identify the location and severity of narrowing within the spine.

Treatment options

Treatment depends on symptom severity and functional impact. In many patients, initial care includes medication, physical therapy and activity modification. In more advanced cases, surgery may be considered to relieve nerve compression and improve quality of life.

Specialist evaluation

If you are experiencing persistent back pain, weakness or difficulty walking, a specialist consultation may help determine whether spinal stenosis is present and which treatment options should be considered.
