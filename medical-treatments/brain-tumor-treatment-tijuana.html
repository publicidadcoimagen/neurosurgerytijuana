Brain tumor evaluation in Tijuana

The term brain tumor is used to describe lesions that may develop within the brain or nearby structures. Some may be benign and others malignant, but in all cases specialist evaluation is important to establish the diagnosis and determine the most appropriate next step.

Many patients search for brain tumor treatment in Tijuana when they experience neurological symptoms such as persistent headaches, seizures, weakness, vision changes or balance problems. These symptoms do not always indicate a tumor, but they do justify careful medical assessment.

Symptoms that may require evaluation

  • Persistent or progressive headache
  • Seizures
  • Weakness or numbness
  • Vision changes
  • Balance or coordination problems
  • New neurological changes

Diagnosis

Evaluation usually includes medical history, neurological examination and imaging studies such as MRI or CT scan. These studies help identify the location, size and general characteristics of the lesion.

Treatment options

Treatment depends on several factors, including lesion type, location, symptoms and the patient’s overall condition. Some cases may require monitoring and follow-up, while others may involve neurosurgical treatment or multidisciplinary care.

Specialist consultation

A specialist consultation helps review prior imaging, explain findings clearly and discuss available options. The goal is to provide accurate information and an appropriate treatment plan for each individual case.
