Chronic Back Pain Evaluation in Tijuana

Chronic back pain evaluation in Tijuana is intended for patients whose low back, mid-back, or neck pain has persisted for weeks or months, interferes with normal activities, or has not improved with usual treatment. The objective is not only to reduce pain, but to understand its cause and determine whether the issue is mechanical, degenerative, neurological, or related to nerve compression.

Chronic back pain is one of the most common reasons patients seek specialist care. In some situations, the cause is relatively mild and can be managed conservatively. In other cases, the pain may reflect disc disease, spinal stenosis, nerve irritation, or a structural issue that deserves more focused evaluation. That is why a careful assessment is so important.

What is considered chronic back pain?

Back pain is generally considered chronic when it continues beyond the expected recovery time or when it returns repeatedly over a prolonged period. This does not always mean that the condition is severe, but it does suggest that a more structured evaluation may be useful, especially if daily function, work, or sleep are affected.

Warning signs that justify specialist assessment

  • Pain that persists despite medication or rest.
  • Pain radiating into the buttock, thigh, or leg.
  • Tingling, numbness, or weakness.
  • Difficulty standing or walking.
  • Pain that repeatedly limits daily life.

How chronic back pain is evaluated

Evaluation begins with a detailed review of where the pain is located, how it behaves, what worsens or relieves it, how long it has been present, and whether it is associated with nerve-related symptoms. This is followed by a physical and neurological examination aimed at identifying signs of nerve involvement, structural limitation, or functional deficit.

Depending on the case, imaging studies such as MRI, CT scan, or X-rays may be reviewed or requested. However, the decision is never based on imaging alone. Many patients have degenerative findings that do not fully explain their symptoms, while others have relatively subtle imaging changes that are clinically very relevant.

Possible causes of chronic back pain

Chronic back pain may be related to degenerative changes, disc problems, spinal stenosis, nerve compression, postural strain, or other structural conditions that deserve individual assessment. The purpose of consultation is to distinguish which cases can be managed conservatively and which ones need more advanced spine or neurosurgical review.

Treatment possibilities

Many patients improve with conservative care such as medication, rehabilitation, activity modification, and follow-up. In some cases, if neurological signs or significant structural issues are present, more specific treatment may need to be considered. This is always decided based on diagnosis rather than pain alone.

Goal of the evaluation

The purpose of chronic back pain evaluation in Tijuana is to give the patient a clearer understanding of what may be causing the pain, which tests are actually useful, and what treatment path makes the most sense. A more precise diagnosis helps avoid repetitive treatments that do not work and supports better decisions moving forward.
