Neurosurgery Services in Tijuana

This section provides information about the main neurosurgery services available in Tijuana with Dr. José Manuel Segovia y Galindo. These services include neurological consultation, neurosurgical consultation, spine surgery, nerve compression treatment, chronic back pain evaluation, microsurgery of the spine, and brain tumor care. Each page explains what the service involves, when it may be useful, and what patients can expect during evaluation.
